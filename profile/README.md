Pact is building an AI treasurer for crypto teams on Solana. Routine payments can move automatically, exceptional payments become a vote in the team chat, and forbidden actions never move money.

## Graduated authority

| Auto | Vote | Never |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Known recipient, inside caps | Valid action above a cap | Unknown or forbidden action |
| Pay and post the receipt | Collect the team's threshold | Refuse and record why |

Our rule is simple: **the agent can propose; only the chain can permit.**

## What we are building

- on-chain treasury policies and spending limits;
- a governed recipient registry with vote, delay, and veto;
- one-tap approvals in Telegram;
- refusal records that remain readable on Solana;
- immediate agent revocation and a team-controlled exit path.

Pact is currently in development. Follow this organization for the protocol, agent, Telegram experience, and technical documentation as they are released.
